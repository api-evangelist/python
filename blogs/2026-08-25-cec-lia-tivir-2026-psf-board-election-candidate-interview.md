Who are you? I am Cecília Tivir, a Mozambican researcher in Artificial Intelligence applied to education, an educator, and an open-source community organizer. Throughout my career in technology, I have worked to build inclusive spaces for women and underrepresented groups; I co-founded the Mozambican Association of Women in Technology (Wansati Lab), organized Django Girls workshops across various Mozambican cities, co-founded the Python Mozambique community, and co-founded the PyLadies chapters in Maputo and Beira.
